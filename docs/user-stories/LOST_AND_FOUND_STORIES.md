# User Stories: Lost & Found & Campus Safety (Issue #2)
> **Track**: Lost Property Protocols, Campus Locations, and Non-Emergency Contacts

---

## Story 1: Lost Backpack / Laptop Reporting
### **As a** student who lost an item on campus (e.g., a laptop at the Richland library),
### **I want to** ask the chatbot where the Lost & Found is located and how to report the item,
### **So that** I can recover my lost property as soon as possible.

#### **Acceptance Criteria**:
1. The chatbot must provide the campus-specific location of the official Lost & Found department (e.g., "The Richland Campus Lost & Found is located at the Campus Police Office, Room D112").
2. It must supply the telephone number and hours of operation for the safety/police desk.
3. The response must instruct the student on the reporting process: *"Please visit the office with a valid ID and be prepared to describe the item in detail."*

---

## Story 2: Non-Emergency Campus Security Contact
### **As a** student or staff member working late on campus,
### **I want to** ask the chatbot for the non-emergency campus security phone number,
### **So that** I can request a safety escort to my vehicle.

#### **Acceptance Criteria**:
1. The chatbot must search the directory for the non-emergency dispatch number (e.g., "972-860-4290").
2. The response must state that the safety dispatch operates 24/7.
3. The response must include a prominent warning to dial 911 (or campus emergency extension 3911) in case of an active emergency.
